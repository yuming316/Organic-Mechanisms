# Nucleophilic Aromatic Substitution

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Replacement of an aromatic leaving group by a nucleophile, activated by electron-withdrawing groups or proceeding through a benzyne intermediate.

## Key steps
- **SNAr**: addition to form the Meisenheimer complex, then elimination of the leaving group.
- **Benzyne route**: dehydrohalogenation to a benzyne, followed by nucleophilic addition.

## Representative examples
- Substitution of ortho/para-nitro aryl halides by amines or alkoxides.
- Chlorobenzene with sodium amide.
