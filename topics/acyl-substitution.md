# Nucleophilic Acyl Substitution

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Replacement of a leaving group on a carboxylic acid derivative by a nucleophile through a tetrahedral intermediate.

## Key steps
1. Nucleophilic attack on the acyl carbonyl.
2. Collapse of the tetrahedral intermediate; elimination of the leaving group.

## Intermediates
- Tetrahedral alkoxide intermediate.

## Relative reactivity
- Acyl halide > acid anhydride > ester > amide.

## Representative examples
- Esterification and transesterification.
- Acid- and base-catalyzed ester hydrolysis.
- Amidation and aminolysis of acyl derivatives.
