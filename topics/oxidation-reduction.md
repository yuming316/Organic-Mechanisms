# Oxidation and Reduction

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Interconversion of functional groups by changing the oxidation state at carbon.

## Oxidation examples
- Primary/secondary alcohols to aldehydes, ketones, or acids.
- Alkene epoxidation and dihydroxylation.

## Reduction examples
- Carbonyls to alcohols.
- Carboxyl derivatives to alcohols or amines.
- Alkene/alkyne hydrogenation.

## Notes
- Track oxidation state at carbon and reagent selectivity (mild vs strong oxidants; chemoselective reducing agents).
