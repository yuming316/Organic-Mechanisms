# Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
An electrophile replaces an aromatic hydrogen; aromaticity is restored by deprotonation.

## Key steps
1. Electrophile generation.
2. Attack to form the Wheland (σ) complex.
3. Rearomatization by deprotonation.

## Directing effects
- Ortho/para directors: activating or weakly deactivating.
- Meta directors: strongly deactivating.

## Representative examples
- Nitration; halogenation; sulfonation.
- Friedel–Crafts alkylation and acylation.
