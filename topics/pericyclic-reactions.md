# Pericyclic Reactions

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Concerted reactions with a cyclic array of moving electrons, classified by orbital symmetry.

## Reaction types
- **Cycloadditions**: e.g., Diels–Alder.
- **Electrocyclic**: ring opening and ring closure.
- **Sigmatropic rearrangements**.

- Apply frontier-orbital and Woodward–Hoffmann rules to predict allowed stereochemistry.
