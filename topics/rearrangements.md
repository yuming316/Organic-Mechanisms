# Molecular Rearrangements

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Migration of a group, bond, or atom to an adjacent center, often driven by an electron-deficient center.

## Representative examples
- Wagner–Meerwein carbocation rearrangement.
- Pinacol rearrangement.
- Beckmann rearrangement.
- Baeyer–Villiger oxidation.
- Claisen and Cope rearrangements.

## Notes
- Track the migrating group, the origin and destination atoms, and how the driving force (relief of strain, more stable cation, carbonyl formation) controls the outcome.
