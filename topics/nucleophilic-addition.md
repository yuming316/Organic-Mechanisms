# Nucleophilic Addition to Carbonyl Compounds

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Addition of a nucleophile to the carbonyl carbon, with the π electrons moving to oxygen.

## Key steps
1. Nucleophilic attack.
2. Proton transfer to the alkoxide.

## Intermediates
- Tetrahedral alkoxide; hemiacetal.

## Reversible vs irreversible addition
- Irreversible: hydride and organometallic reagents.
- Reversible: imine and acetal formation.

## Representative examples
- NaBH₄ / LiAlH₄ reduction.
- Grignard and organolithium addition.
- Imine formation; acetal formation.
