# Electrophilic Addition to Alkenes and Alkynes

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Addition of an electrophile across a π bond, followed by capture of the resulting cation or bridged intermediate.

## Key steps
1. The π bond attacks the electrophile; Markovnikov orientation generally applies.
2. Capture by a nucleophile (or rearrangement before capture).

## Intermediates
- Carbocation.
- Bridged halonium ion.

## Representative examples
- HX addition; halogenation.
- Acid-catalyzed hydration.
- Oxymercuration–demercuration.
- Hydroboration–oxidation.
