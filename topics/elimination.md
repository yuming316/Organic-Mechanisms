# Elimination Reactions

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Removal of two substituents to form a new π bond (alkene or alkyne).

## Key steps
- **E1**: ionization to a carbocation, then deprotonation.
- **E2**: concerted anti-periplanar deprotonation and leaving-group departure.
- **E1cb**: deprotonation to a carbanion, then leaving-group departure.

## Regioselectivity and stereochemistry
- Zaitsev vs Hofmann products.
- Anti elimination favors the trans/E alkene.

## Representative examples
- Dehydrohalogenation of alkyl halides.
- Acid-catalyzed dehydration of alcohols.
