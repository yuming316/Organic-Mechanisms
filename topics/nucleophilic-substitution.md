# Nucleophilic Substitution at Saturated Carbon

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Replacement of a leaving group at an sp³ carbon by an electron-pair donor, classified by rate law and mechanism.

## Key steps
- **SN2**: concerted backside attack of the nucleophile; the leaving group departs in the same step.
- **SN1**: leaving-group departure gives a carbocation, followed by nucleophile capture.
- **SN1′ (allylic)**: substitution with rearrangement of the allylic double bond.
- **Neighboring-group participation**: intramolecular assistance through a cyclic intermediate.

## Intermediates / transition states
- SN2: pentacoordinate transition state.
- SN1: carbocation and ion pair.

## Stereochemical outcome
- SN2: Walden inversion.
- SN1: racemization, often partial.

## Representative examples
- Hydrolysis and halide exchange of primary and secondary alkyl halides.
- Solvolysis of tertiary alkyl halides.
