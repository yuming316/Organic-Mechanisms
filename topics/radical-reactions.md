# Radical Reactions

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
Reactions proceeding through radical intermediates in an initiation–propagation–termination sequence.

## Key steps
- **Initiation**: bond homolysis by light, heat, or a radical initiator.
- **Propagation**: abstraction and addition steps that regenerate a radical.
- **Termination**: radical–radical combination.

## Representative examples
- Radical halogenation of alkanes.
- Peroxide-mediated radical HBr addition (anti-Markovnikov).
- SRN1 nucleophilic radical substitution.
