# Condensation and Coupling Reactions

> Outline placeholder — mechanism details and diagrams to be added.

## Scope
C–C bond formation through enolates, enamines, or related carbonyl nucleophiles, often with loss of a small molecule.

## Representative examples
- Aldol addition and condensation.
- Claisen condensation.
- Knoevenagel condensation.
- Michael addition.
- Wittig olefination.
